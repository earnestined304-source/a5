  • Advance Confirmation: A reservation is confirmed only upon receipt of formal written confirmation and processing of required reservation fees through authorized payment gateways.

Our supper club gatherings are founded upon mutual respect, thoughtful conversation, and communal conviviality. To maintain an exceptional atmosphere for all attendees, guests agree to adhere to our Code of Conduct:
