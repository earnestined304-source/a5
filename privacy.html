DinnerChapter Privacy Policy & Data Stewardship Standards

Last Updated & Verified: September 5, 2026 • Compliant with Global Privacy Regulations (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, CalOPPA)

At DinnerChapter Atelier LLC ('DinnerChapter', 'we', 'our', or 'us'), protecting the personal privacy and data integrity of our supper club guests, digital subscribers, culinary consulting clients, and website visitors is a fundamental organizational commitment. This comprehensive Privacy Policy outlines the categories of data we collect, our technical security protocols, the specific purposes for data processing, and your statutory rights regarding personal data under applicable data privacy frameworks.

1. Information We Collect and Modes of Collection

We collect personal information through direct interactions on our digital platforms and automated telemetry necessary to deliver secure, responsive browsing experiences:

  • Directly Provided Information: When you complete our contact forms, register for a seasonal supper club seating, subscribe to our culinary research dispatch, or request custom menu consulting, you provide your name, email address, telephone contact, dietary preferences, and event specifications.
  • Culinary and Dietary Data: In connection with dinner reservations, guests may disclose food allergies, intolerance specifics, or religious dietary requirements. This information is processed strictly for the health and culinary safety of attendees during dinner events.
  • Automated Device and Telemetry Data: When you access our digital platform, our servers log standard Internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser user-agent strings, operating system parameters, referring URLs, access timestamps, and page interaction velocity.
  • Analytics and Cookie Telemetry: We utilize minimal, privacy-centric analytics (including Google Analytics 4 tag G-0LY0HY7L01) to evaluate content performance, dwell times, and user navigation paths without constructing permanent personal surveillance profiles.

2. Legal Basis and Purposes of Data Processing

DinnerChapter processes collected personal data under explicit, lawful bases established under global data protection frameworks:

  1. Contractual Performance: To process supper club reservations, confirm seating arrangements, fulfill custom recipe consultations, and communicate logistical details regarding private dinner events.
  2. Consent: To deliver our optional monthly culinary science journal, recipe releases, and supper club announcements when you voluntarily opt in. You maintain the right to withdraw consent at any time via one-click unsubscribe mechanisms.
  3. Legitimate Interests: To maintain platform security, detect malicious automated intrusion attempts, optimize page loading efficiency across global content delivery networks, and refine our gastronomic educational offerings.
  4. Legal Compliance and Food Safety: To maintain accurate tax and accounting records, comply with municipal hospitality ordinances, and document allergen mitigation protocols.

3. Absolute Policy Against Commercial Data Monetization

DinnerChapter does not sell, rent, lease, trade, or monetize your personal information to third-party data brokers, behavioral advertising exchanges, or external commercial entities under any circumstances. We believe that hospitality is grounded in mutual trust, and your personal details are treated with the highest confidentiality.

Information is shared strictly with vetted operational infrastructure service providers who operate under contractual data processing agreements, including secure cloud hosting providers, encrypted database storage facilities, and certified transactional email delivery gateways.

4. Data Retention and Deletion Schedules

We retain personal information only for the duration strictly necessary to fulfill the specific operational objectives for which it was gathered. Dietary preferences and allergen notes submitted for specific supper club dinners are purged from active service records within thirty (30) days following the conclusion of the event, unless a client explicitly requests retention for recurring private catering arrangements.

General correspondence, contact inquiries, and email newsletter subscription records are maintained until an explicit deletion request is received or until an account remains inactive for a continuous period of twenty-four (24) months.

5. Technical and Organizational Data Security Measures

DinnerChapter employs robust enterprise-grade security protocols to protect all stored and transmitted data against unauthorized access, destruction, alteration, or disclosure:

  • End-to-End Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3): All network traffic between your web browser and our servers is encrypted using modern cryptographic ciphers.
  • Restricted Least-Privilege Access: Access to user contact lists and reservation records is restricted strictly to authorized culinary management personnel who are bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Automated Vulnerability Auditing: Our web infrastructure undergoes routine automated security patch updates, firewall monitoring, and static code analysis to eliminate security vulnerabilities.

6. Your Statutory Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Depending on your geographic jurisdiction, you possess statutory legal rights concerning your personal information:

  • Right of Access: You may request a verified copy of all personal data records maintained by DinnerChapter concerning your identity.
  • Right to Rectification: You have the right to correct inaccurate, obsolete, or incomplete personal data records.
  • Right to Erasure ('Right to Be Forgotten'): You may request the permanent deletion of your personal records from our active servers and backup archives.
  • Right to Restrict or Object to Processing: You may object to the processing of your personal information for direct marketing or analytical research.
  • Right to Data Portability: You may request your personal data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format (such as JSON or CSV).

To exercise any of your statutory privacy rights, submit a data inquiry, or request clarification regarding our data governance protocols, please direct formal communication to our dedicated privacy desk:
