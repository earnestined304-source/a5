DinnerChapter Cookie Policy & User Tracking Governance

Last Updated & Verified: September 5, 2026 • Compliant with EU ePrivacy Directive & GDPR Standards

DinnerChapter Atelier LLC ('DinnerChapter', 'we', 'our', or 'us') uses cookies, local browser storage tokens, and related web telemetry tools on dinnerchapter.com to ensure reliable platform performance, remember your aesthetic preferences (such as our Twilight / Candlelight theme switcher), and analyze aggregate reader engagement with our culinary publications.

1. What Are Cookies and Web Storage Tokens?

A cookie is a compact alphanumeric text file transmitted by a web server and stored locally within your web browser. When you revisit our platform or navigate between pages, the cookie allows our infrastructure to recognize your device, maintain your theme selection, and optimize page delivery speeds.

Modern web browsers also support HTML5 LocalStorage, which allows client-side scripts to store small preference tokens (such as light/dark mode choices) directly on your device without transmitting data back to the server on every HTTP request. This enhances client browsing speed while preserving privacy.

2. Categories of Cookies Deployed on DinnerChapter

We classify all cookies utilized across our digital properties into three functional tiers:

Cookie Category Purpose & Functional Role Persistence Lifespan Essential vs. Optional
Strictly Necessary Maintains core navigation security, CSRF protection, and load balancing across server nodes. Session (Closed on browser exit) Essential (Cannot be disabled)
Functional Preferences Stores your chosen visual aesthetic (Candlelight Warm vs. Twilight Velvet mode in localStorage). 12 Months (Persistent) Functional (User-controlled)
Performance Analytics Google Analytics 4 (Tag: G-0LY0HY7L01) telemetry measuring aggregate pageviews, dwell time, and scroll depth. 24 Months (Persistent) Optional (Can be blocked via browser)

3. Zero Third-Party Advertising Trackers

DinnerChapter maintains an ad-free editorial standard. We do not embed third-party advertising network trackers, behavioral retargeting pixels (such as Meta Pixel or social media ad pixels), or cross-site data harvesting scripts. Our analytics cookies collect strictly anonymized, aggregated metrics designed to help our editorial team understand which culinary research topics resonate most with our readership.

4. Managing and Disabling Cookies in Your Browser

You have complete control over how cookies and local storage tokens are handled on your personal device. You can configure your browser to notify you when cookies are placed, block specific third-party cookies, or purge all stored cache data:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Third-party cookies → Block third-party cookies.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Enhanced Tracking Protection → Strict Mode.
  • Apple Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Prevent cross-site tracking & Block all cookies.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies.

5. Do Not Track (DNT) and Global Privacy Control (GPC) Signals

DinnerChapter respects modern privacy automation standards. If your web browser transmits a Do Not Track ('DNT') or Global Privacy Control ('GPC') signal, our platform automatically honors that signal by suppressing non-essential analytics cookies and telemetry pings for your browsing session.
